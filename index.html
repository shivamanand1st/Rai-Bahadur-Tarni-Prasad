Rai Bahadur Tarni Prasad (1840s - 1913)

Son of Munshi Girdhari Lal, Tarni Prasad hailed from Rohini, Deoghar. A Pleader of higher grade at the Bhagalpur bar (enrolled 1867), he was awarded the title of Rai Bahadur on June 3, 1899, for his outstanding public service, later serving in the Bengal Legislative Council.

The Estates & Zamindari

The family held extensive mokrari istmrari rights across the Bhagalpur division. Key properties included:

Village Matia

Thana No. 168 | Tauzi No. 445

Fell into the share of Rai Saheb Bhuvaneshwar Prasad. Home to the historic pucca house built by Tarni Prasad.

Village Anantpur

Approx. 6500 acres

Fell into the share of Babu Kameshwar Prasad. Featured a major estate residence.
