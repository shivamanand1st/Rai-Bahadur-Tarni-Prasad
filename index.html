Rai Bahadur Tarini Prasad (c. 1843)

Distinguished lawyer in Bhagalpur, member of the Bengal Legislative Council, and a founding trustee of T.N.B. College.

Lineage & Heritage

Part of the prominent Parvatiyar Ambastha Kayastha lineage, extending from the ancestral base and historic bungalow in Rohini, Deoghar.
